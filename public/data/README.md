# Globe geography

`land.geojson` is Natural Earth's 1:110m land dataset, distributed in the public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The globe illustrates research perspectives. City connections do not represent offices, live market feeds, or investment recommendations.
